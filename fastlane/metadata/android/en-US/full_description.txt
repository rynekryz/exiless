Exiless shows what your photos and videos reveal (location, camera, serial numbers, dates, author) and removes it without re-encoding, so quality stays identical.

• JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC/AVIF and MP4/MOV
• Batch cleaning and a share target
• Keeps orientation, colour profiles and animations
• Save to a folder you choose, or share directly
• One UI design, dark mode, themed icon
• No permissions, no network, no tracking

Free and open source (GPL-3.0-or-later).
